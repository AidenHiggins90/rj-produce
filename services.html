Wholesale distribution

High-volume supply you can count on

RJ Produce is a full-service grower–shipper and wholesale distributor of fresh fruits and vegetables, supplying retailers, foodservice operators, and regional distributors across the U.S. and Mexico. Operating from our Pharr, Texas facility — minutes from the border — we move high volume quickly and hold cold-chain integrity from the moment product leaves the field.

Most customers come to us tired of juggling separate suppliers and schedules. We fold it into one relationship: a single point of contact, deliveries timed to your dock, a quality check on every load before it ships, and reorder history in one dashboard.

Cross-border sourcing

A border-region edge no one else has

Pharr, Texas is one of the busiest produce crossing points in North America. We source directly from Mexican growers, clear customs faster than distributors operating from the interior, and put fresher product in front of U.S. buyers at prices that reflect the savings.

  • Harvest volume committed before the season opens — supply locked in, not chased on the spot market
  • Crossings handled by people who clear this border every day
  • Same-day arrival into our Pharr facility, so cold chain never breaks
