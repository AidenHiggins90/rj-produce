Inspection

Every load is checked before it ships

Product is inspected on arrival at our Pharr facility and again before it leaves. Grade, size, pack, and temperature are recorded against the specification on your order, and anything outside spec is pulled rather than shipped and credited later.

  • Receiving inspection with temperature recorded at the door
  • Grade and pack verified against your written spec
  • Out-of-spec product rejected at the dock, not passed downstream
  • Pre-ship check recorded and filed against the lot

Traceability

From your pallet back to the block, in one step

Traceability only matters if it is fast under pressure. Ours runs on the lot code printed on the case.

1

Lot assigned

Each incoming load gets a lot code tied to the grower, growing block, and harvest date.

2

Printed on the case

The code travels on the carton, so the pallet in your warehouse identifies itself without a phone call.

3

Linked to your order

Shipment records connect that lot to the purchase order, the truck, and the delivery date.

4

Recalled in one query

Given a lot, we can name every customer who received it — and given a customer, every lot they hold.

If something goes wrong

Recall procedure

What happens, in what order, and who calls whom.

Hour 0

Hold and notify

Affected lots are placed on hold in our facility and every customer who received them is contacted directly — by phone first, in writing immediately after.

Same day

Scope and document

We identify the full distribution of the lot, notify the grower and the relevant authority, and issue written notice with the lot codes, quantities, and delivery dates involved.

Follow-up

Retrieve and correct

Product is retrieved or destroyed to your requirement, credit is issued, and the corrective action taken with the grower is documented back to you.
