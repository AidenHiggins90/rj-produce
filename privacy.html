Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact us, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

Draft for review. This describes how the website actually handles data today, but it has not been reviewed by counsel — have your attorney check it before you rely on it, particularly if you take business from California or EU-based buyers.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

RJ Produce, Inc. is a grower–shipper of fresh fruits and vegetables based in Pharr, Texas, and part of the GrubMarket family of companies. You can reach us at (956) 781-4000 or 9005 Travis Drive, Pharr, TX 78577.

What this website collects

This is a business website. We do not sell to consumers through it and we do not ask for payment details anywhere on it.

  • Quote requests. When you submit the form on our contact page, we receive the name, company, work email, and phone number you enter, plus the business type, volume, products, and any message you include.
  • Traffic measurement. We use Vercel Web Analytics, which records page views and referrers in aggregate. It sets no cookies and does not build a profile of you or follow you to other sites.
  • Server logs. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard request logs that include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising trackers, and this site sets no cookies of its own.

How we use it

Quote requests are used to price your business and respond to you. We route each request to the sales desk that handles your segment. We do not sell contact details, and we do not add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote — if we ever start sending a newsletter, it will be something you opt into.

Who else sees it

  • Vercel hosts the site and processes requests to it.
  • Resend delivers the quote request to our team and the confirmation to you.
  • GrubMarket, our parent company, where a request needs to be handled by an affiliate.

We disclose information otherwise only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Quote requests live in our email and account records for as long as we have an active or prospective business relationship with you, and afterwards for the period our records retention and tax obligations require.

Your choices

Email or call us and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you are, you may have a formal right to those things — we will honor the request either way rather than argue about which law applies.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and quote requests are transmitted encrypted. No system is perfect; do not send us anything genuinely sensitive through a web form.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and is not directed to children.

Changes

If we change how this works, we will update this page and the date at the top.
