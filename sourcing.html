U.S. & Mexico sourcing

Rooted in two of the world's most productive regions

RJ Produce sources directly from growing regions across the United States and Mexico. Our location in Pharr, Texas puts us at the intersection of two of the world's most productive agricultural nations — we tap both markets at once, so a single relationship covers what a shipper working from one country cannot.

U.S. farms provide reliable domestic supply; Mexican agriculture brings unmatched depth of seasonal variety, volume, and value. Together they give partners year-round access to a broader range than either country alone could support. Produce picked in northern Mexico can reach our facility the same day it crosses — and every hour saved is shelf life on the other end.

We work that side of the border from inside it. Alongside Pharr, RJ Produce operates facilities in Veracruz and Tijuana, which keeps our team among the growers through the season instead of meeting the fruit at the bridge.

Sustainability

Working with nature, not against it

We follow each region's natural growing calendar rather than push farms to force a crop out of season. Because we buy directly from growers, more of what you pay stays in farming communities on both sides of the border instead of going to intermediaries.

Fewer hand-offs also means less handling and less waste — product moves from the field to the cross-dock to your truck, not through a chain of warehouses. Sustainability at RJ Produce isn't a campaign; it's how we've operated since 2004.
