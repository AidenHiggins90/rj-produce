Produce is still a relationship business

Software places the order, but somebody has to know your dock, your spec, and which grower is having a hard week. Here's who that is at RJ Produce.

Founder

Rick Gorena

Rick started RJ Produce in Pharr in 2004 with a handful of grower relationships and a conviction that the produce business runs on trust. Two decades later the company ships to national retail, but the operating principle hasn't changed: show up, keep your word, and treat growers and buyers as long-term partners.

He still knows the growers by name, which is why the calendar holds when the market gets tight.
