Produce is still a relationship business

Software places the order, but somebody has to know your dock, your spec, and which grower is having a hard week. Here's who that is at RJ Produce.

Founder

Rick Gorena

Rick started RJ Produce in Pharr in 2004 with a handful of grower relationships and a conviction that the produce business runs on trust. Two decades later the company ships to national retail, but the operating principle hasn't changed: show up, keep your word, and treat growers and buyers as long-term partners.

He still knows the growers by name, which is why the calendar holds when the market gets tight.

Who you work with

Four people stand behind your account

Every account is assigned a named contact in each of these roles at setup. You'll have their direct line, not a queue.

Your account manager

Owns pricing, the order calendar, and the relationship. Flags seasonal changes before they hit your shelf and is the one number you call when something is off.

Sourcing

Works the grower side on both sides of the border, commits harvest volume ahead of the season, and finds the substitute when weather takes a region out.

Quality assurance

Inspects incoming and outgoing loads against your written spec, holds the certification paperwork, and handles documentation requests for your vendor onboarding.

Logistics

Runs the crossings and the cross-dock, books the freight, and keeps delivery windows matched to your receiving hours.

Rooted in the Valley

A Rio Grande Valley company

Our team lives in the region we source from. That means the grower relationships aren't managed at arm's length from a corporate office — they're neighbors, and the reputation we carry locally is the same one buyers are trusting.

It also means we're straight with you about what a season actually looks like. If a crop is short, you'll hear it from us before it shows up as a gap on your shelf.
