Who we are

A Rio Grande Valley grower–shipper, built on trust

RJ Produce started in 2004 in Pharr, Texas with a handful of grower relationships and a belief that the produce business runs on trust. In a region where most operators chased the lowest price, we bet on showing up, keeping our word, and treating growers and buyers as long-term partners.

Those early relationships became the foundation. As farms on both sides of the border learned they could count on us season after season, the business grew — from local deliveries to supplying branded product to some of the largest retailers in the country. In 2021 that track record brought us into the GrubMarket family, adding national reach and technology without changing how we do business.
